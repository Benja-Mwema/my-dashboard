Same-bar M15 ordering cannot be established from OHLC; only later-bar contacts are used for origin-to-target chronology. Failure requires the stated completed H4 close, not an intrabar wick.
M15 qualification and trigger, M30 structure, H1 permission, H4 allowance and confirmation-time no-chase would all be needed to evaluate a fresh trade. They have NOT been fully replayed here.

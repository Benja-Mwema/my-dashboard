Trend and pullback

EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200. Three-bar ATR-normalized slopes S20 and S50 are negative; S200 is nonpositive. EMA20–50 separation is 0.25–2 ATR. A qualifying touch has High ≥ EMA20 and Close ≤ EMA50. A new touch run starts an episode only after a non-touch candle whose close was below its EMA20.

SELL confirmation

Within three bars of the latest qualifying touch: bearish body ≥0.4 ATR; close in the lower 35%; fresh close below EMA10 after the preceding close was at or above EMA10; extension below EMA20 >0 and ≤1 ATR. Six contiguous M30 bars are required. No BUY model is deployed.

Episode and outcome contract

First passing candle consumes the episode. Signal index distance must exceed 12 bars. Consecutive touches update the last-touch time without clearing consumption. Trend failure, expiry or a timestamp gap invalidates the episode; a gap also resets cooldown. Entry is the next contiguous M30 open. A missing next bar is pending; an observed gap cancels the entry.

One pip = 0.0001; one point = 0.00001. Primary outcome: Close[i+12] relative to Open[i+1]. No protective stop, take profit, break-even, position size or live execution is defined. EMA and Wilder ATR seeds use the first source candle in January 2025. Native broker timestamps are preserved; historical conversion to EAT is unverified.

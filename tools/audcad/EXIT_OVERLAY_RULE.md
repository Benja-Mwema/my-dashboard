# AUDCAD_EXIT_RETRACE50_V1_0

User-authorized 13 November 2026. Separate research exit and chart overlay; AUDCAD_H1_BUY_V1_0 and AUDCAD_H1_SELL_V1_0 entry predicates and original frozen TP60/SL60 outcome fields are unchanged. Default dashboard view is this new overlay, with frozen baseline available in Exit rules. Not validated for live trading.

Journey = running maximum favorable distance from the benchmark entry. Confirm a turn at a completed M30 close when directional profit < half that maximum. Thus exactly 50% is not a turn. With no favorable excursion, a negative completed close is early failure. Completed-close confirmation is the declared causal reconstruction convention; no intrabar turn time is claimed.

TP remains +60 pips, closing the opportunity. SL remains -60 pips as the outer adverse barrier. At the turn, maximum favorable movement >=20 and <60 arms BE prospectively; the next return to entry closes at zero gross pips. Exactly20 belongs to this protection branch; the frozen descriptive movement-success label still requires strictly >20. If the confirming close is already beyond entry on the losing side, report AMBIGUOUS_BE_ACTIVATION, not a fabricated retroactive BE fill. If a later opening gaps through an armed BE, use its observed opening reference, label BE_GAP, and disclose gross loss rather than promise a fill at entry.

A turn with maximum favorable movement <20 sets EARLY_TURN. After that, close at SL60 or the next opposite qualified H1 confirmation, whichever is first. Opposite confirmation uses the M30 open at the signal's availability time; there is no carry to a missing bar. An already-triggered barrier at that opening takes precedence. TP60 can still occur while waiting. If this opportunity subsequently reaches >=20 without exiting and later completes another >50% retracement, it upgrades to BE protection. No retrospective opposite exit is created before turn confirmation.

Unknown within-M30 ordering of TP and SL, or TP and armed BE, is AMBIGUOUS. Nominal barrier outcomes are research references, not fills. All exits stop overlay P/L accumulation; open marks and uncertain cases remain separate. Overlay rate is nonnegative known closed outcomes divided by all known closed outcomes, explicitly including BE; it is not the frozen TP win rate.
